Google Classroom emergency overlay

The Google Classroom disguise is rendered in HTML by global-utils.js. It randomly picks fictional teacher names each time the overlay opens and uses four freely available Unsplash portraits:

- Vitaly Gariev: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-female-teacher-smiling-at-the-desk-with-a-laptop-TOuMSV0Nqjk
- Troy Spoelma: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-woman-with-long-brown-hair-smiling-at-the-camera-3paKXHWzsDw
- Luke Thornton: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-man-looks-directly-at-the-camera-Vwgy_Xl94kc
- Brooke Balentine: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-smiling-woman-poses-for-a-portrait-cdZcNIca4w0

Portraits are loaded from images.unsplash.com and are free to use under the Unsplash License. The displayed teacher names are fictional.

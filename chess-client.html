Play Chess over your local network

No account, token, game server or backend. One player creates a named room and shares a compact, compressed invitation code; the other joins with that code. Moves travel directly between browsers over WebRTC.

1. Host a game

After your friend joins, ask them to send you their answer code.

2. Join a game

Create a room to host, or paste a friend's invitation to join.

Backend-free connection note: browsers cannot scan Wi-Fi for other browser rooms. The invite/answer exchange is the signaling step WebRTC needs; after that, chess moves go peer-to-peer on the local network. Both players must be on the same LAN, allow local peer connections, and keep this page open. This uses no STUN or relay server.

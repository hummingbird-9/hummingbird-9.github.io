Play people online

No Lichess account, Chess.com account, API token, or sign-in is needed for the guest options below. Choose a chess site and play using its official game board.

Lichess

Play anonymously against the community, or create a private one-game invitation and send its link to a friend.

  • Choose Create lobby game to be matched with another player.
  • Choose Challenge a friend to create a shareable invitation link.
  • The first person who opens a friend-invite link joins the game; they do not need an account.
Open Lichess Guest Play ↗

Chess.com

Use Chess.com's official Guest Play to get matched anonymously with another guest at a similar selected skill level.

  • Stay signed out (or use a private window).
  • Choose Play Online, select your skill level, then choose Play as Guest.
  • Choose a time control and Chess.com matches you with another guest.
Open Chess.com Guest Play ↗
Why the games open on their own sites: Lichess's Board API is for authenticated accounts, but its normal website allows anonymous games and friend-invite links. Chess.com's published API is read-only, while its own Guest Play feature handles anonymous matchmaking. Keeping play on each provider's official site avoids needing a token or using an unofficial move-control integration.

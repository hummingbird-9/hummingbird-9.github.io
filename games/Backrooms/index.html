TheBackrooms

Level 0 · non-euclidean office space

You noclipped out of reality and landed in the wallpaper. Roughly six hundred million square miles of empty office, lit by the buzz of fluorescent lights at maximum hum. You can only see the room you are standing in — and something spawned in the next one. Find the wall that glows. Walk into it.

No entry on record.

How to survive

  • Move — WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, hold your left thumb anywhere on the left of the screen and steer.
  • Run — Shift or space. On a phone, shove the stick to the edge, or hold a second finger on the right. Running burns the bar at the bottom, and running is loud.
  • Sight — you see one room at a time, plus a sliver through your own doorways. The rest of the floor is not there until you walk into it.
  • Sound — the growl gets louder the closer it gets, and you can hear your own heart: it starts up when the thing comes within about a room of you and climbs the nearer it gets, wall or no wall. If your pulse is racing, so should you be. Wear headphones. Regret it.
  • The way out — one wall on each floor glows. Walk into it and it lets you through. Level 0 drops into the poolrooms; the poolrooms let you out.
  • Entities — Captain Clark stalks the office and never gets bored. Bacteria is faster in the water but loses interest sooner.
